Read this first

Calories come from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with your activity level, and food values are typical averages for the serving sizes shown. Real foods and recipes vary, so treat every number as an estimate and adjust by how you feel and what the scale does over a few weeks.

This is general guidance, not medical advice. It is for healthy adults and is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with conditions such as diabetes, kidney disease, thyroid problems or a history of eating disorders. If any of these apply, or you take regular medicines, ask a doctor or registered dietitian before changing what you eat.
